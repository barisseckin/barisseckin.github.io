# Delete your Glowdrobe AI account

This page explains how to delete your account in **Glowdrobe AI** (developer: Barış Seçkin) and what happens to your data.

## Option 1 — in the app

1. Open Glowdrobe AI and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile → Settings → Delete Account**.
3. Confirm. You are signed out immediately.

## Option 2 — without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email **[contact@glowdrobe.ai](mailto:contact@glowdrobe.ai?subject=Delete%20my%20Glowdrobe%20account)** **from the email address of your Glowdrobe account** with the subject "Delete my Glowdrobe account". If you signed in with Apple and hid your email, include your Apple private-relay address. We confirm the request by email and process it within 7 days.

## What is deleted

After a **14-day grace period** (sign in again within it if you change your mind) we permanently delete:

- your profile, username, profile photo and style preferences;
- your wardrobe items and photos, outfits, AI stylist results and virtual try-on images;
- your posts, stories, comments, likes, follows and chat messages;
- your push notification tokens, sign-in links (Google / Apple) and remaining AI credits.

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Purchase and subscription records** (store transaction / order IDs, product, dates, credits granted) are kept only where Turkish tax and commercial law requires it (up to **10 years**), and only for that purpose. They are not used for anything else.
- Nothing else is kept after the grace period.

Deleting your account does **not** cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the store first — see [Support](/support).

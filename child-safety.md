# Child Safety Standards — Glowdrobe AI

**Last updated:** 2026-10-30

Glowdrobe AI (developer: Barış Seçkin) is a wardrobe, outfit and social app with AI features, chat, posts, comments and stories. This page sets out our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and how we enforce them.

## 1. Zero tolerance

We have **zero tolerance** for child sexual abuse and exploitation. Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers minors is prohibited, and accounts involved in it are permanently removed.

## 2. Prohibited content and behaviour

The following are strictly prohibited anywhere in the App — in posts, stories, comments, profiles, chat messages, uploaded photos and AI requests:

- child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, edited or AI-generated imagery;
- sexualised images, comments or descriptions of minors;
- grooming, sextortion, or any attempt to contact a minor for sexual purposes or to obtain images from them;
- using AI features (such as virtual try-on or the AI stylist) to create sexualised content involving minors or anyone who appears to be a minor;
- sharing, requesting, advertising or linking to any of the above.

## 3. Age requirement

You must be **at least 13 years old** (or 16 in EEA countries where that is the minimum age) to use Glowdrobe AI. The App is not directed to children under these ages. Accounts we find to belong to children under the minimum age are removed.

## 4. In-app reporting and blocking

- Every post, comment, story, profile and chat message has a **Report** option, and AI-generated results (virtual try-on images, AI stylist answers) can be reported as well.
- You can **block** any user at any time to stop interacting with them.
- Reports are sent to our moderation team and are **reviewed within 24 hours**. Reports concerning the safety of a minor are treated with the highest priority.

## 5. Moderation and enforcement

- Text and images posted publicly or sent in chat are automatically screened by an AI moderation service to detect objectionable content, including sexual content involving minors, before or shortly after they become visible.
- Our AI providers apply safety filters to AI-generated images and text, and any AI output can be reported by users.
- When we find CSAE content or behaviour, we remove the content, permanently ban the accounts involved and preserve relevant information as required by law.

## 6. Reporting to authorities

We report apparent child sexual abuse material and child exploitation to the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** and/or the competent **local law enforcement authorities**, and we cooperate with lawful requests from authorities investigating such cases.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. You can also report CSAM directly to NCMEC at [report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org) or to your national hotline (see [inhope.org](https://www.inhope.org)).

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I manage or cancel my Premium subscription?**
Premium is billed by Apple. On your iPhone open **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Glowdrobe**, or tap **Manage subscription** on the Premium screen in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

**How do I get a refund?**
Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**I bought something but it doesn't show up.**
Open the Premium screen and tap **Restore Purchases** while signed in to the same Glowdrobe account you used to buy. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of purchase (no card details needed).

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app go to **Profile → Settings → Delete Account**. Your account is deleted after a 14-day grace period. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

**How do I report a user or content?**
Use the **⋯** menu on any post, comment, story, profile or chat and choose **Report**, or **Block** the user. Our team reviews reports within 24 hours.

**How do I access or delete my personal data?**
Email us from the address linked to your account. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) for your rights under KVKK and GDPR.

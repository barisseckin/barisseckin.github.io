# Glowdrobe AI Support

We're happy to help. Email us at **[baris.seckin@glowdrobe.ai](mailto:baris.seckin@glowdrobe.ai)** — we reply within 2 business days (reports of abusive content are reviewed within 24 hours).

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I manage or cancel my Premium subscription?**
Tap **Manage subscription** on the Premium screen in the app, or:

- **iPhone / iPad (App Store):** open **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Glowdrobe**. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.
- **Android (Google Play):** open the **Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Glowdrobe**. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

**How do I get a refund?**
Purchases are processed by the store you bought from: App Store refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com); Google Play refunds by Google — see [Google Play refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

**I bought something but it doesn't show up.**
Open the Premium screen and tap **Restore Purchases** while signed in to the same Glowdrobe account you used to buy. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of purchase (no card details needed).

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app go to **Profile → Settings → Delete Account**. Your account is deleted after a 14-day grace period. You can also request deletion from any browser: see [Delete your account](/delete-account). Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription — cancel it in the store (see above).

**How do I report a user or content?**
Use the **⋯** menu on any post, comment, story, profile or chat and choose **Report**, or **Block** the user. Our team reviews reports within 24 hours.

**How do I access or delete my personal data?**
Email us from the address linked to your account. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) for your rights under KVKK and GDPR.

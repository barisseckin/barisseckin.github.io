## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account data

- Email address, name and username.
- If you sign in with **Google** or **Apple**, the account identifier provided by that service and, if you choose to share them, your name and email. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, Apple provides us with a private relay email address.
- If you register with email, your password is stored only in hashed form.

### 2.2 Profile data

- Profile photo, bio and any other optional profile details you add.

### 2.3 User content

- Photos of wardrobe items and their attributes (e.g., category, color, season).
- Outfits and combinations you create.
- Posts, comments, stories, polls and chat messages (including voice messages, if you use that feature).

### 2.4 Photos for virtual try-on and AI features

- Selfies or body photos you upload to use the virtual try-on feature, and the images generated from them.
- See Section 5 for how these photos are processed.

### 2.5 Approximate location

- Approximate (city-level) location, used to provide weather-based outfit suggestions.
- Location is accessed only while you are using the App and only if you grant permission. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

### 2.6 Device push token

- A device token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, used to send you push notifications (e.g., new messages or comments). You can turn off notifications in your device settings.

### 2.7 Purchase records

- Apple transaction identifiers and the status of your subscription and in-app purchases.
- **Payments are processed by Apple.** We never see or store your card or payment details.

### 2.8 Technical logs

- Technical and diagnostic data such as device type, operating system, app version, error reports and server logs (which may include IP address and request timestamps), used to keep the App secure and working.

### 2.9 Device permissions

The App asks for your permission before accessing:

- **Camera:** to take photos of clothing, posts, stories and selfies for virtual try-on.
- **Photo Library:** to choose photos to upload and to save images (e.g., try-on results) to your device.
- **Microphone:** to record voice messages in chat.
- **Location (while using the App):** for weather-based outfit suggestions.
- **Notifications:** to send push notifications.

You can withdraw these permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features may not work without them.

### 2.10 What we do not do

- We do **not** use advertising SDKs or show ads based on your data.
- We do **not** track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 5. AI Features and Your Photos

- **Consent first:** before you use an AI feature that processes your photos for the first time, the App asks for your explicit consent.
- **What is sent:** to produce the result you request, the relevant photos and wardrobe data are sent to our AI providers:
  - **OpenAI** — AI stylist text and image generation, and content moderation.
  - **NanoBanana** — virtual try-on image generation.
- **No model training:** data is sent only to produce the result you requested. Under OpenAI's API terms, data submitted through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models.
- **Withdrawing consent:** you can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at barisseckin0@gmail.com, or by deleting the relevant content or your account. After withdrawal, AI features that process your photos will no longer be available to you.

## 10. Account Deletion

- You can delete your account in the App at **Settings → Delete Account**.
- After you request deletion, there is a **14-day grace period**. After this period, your account and personal data are permanently deleted, except where retention is legally required (e.g., purchase and tax records).
- If you signed in with Apple, your Sign in with Apple tokens are revoked when your account is deleted.
- Deleting your account does not cancel an active App Store subscription. Please cancel it in your App Store account settings.
